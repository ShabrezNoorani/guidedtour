Meet Your Passionate Paris Guides

We believe a tour is only as good as the guide. At Scenic Zest, our guides aren't just experts; they're storytellers dedicated to making your Parisian journey unforgettable.

Shabrez

Founder & Lead Guide

Hailing from the beautiful valley of Kashmir, I brought my passion for stories and hospitality to the heart of Paris. After completing my Master's in Business, I realized my true calling wasn't in an office, but on the historic streets of this magical city, sharing its secrets with travelers from around the world.

For me, a tour is not just about facts and dates; it's about weaving a narrative that connects you to the soul of Paris. I believe in making history come alive, one story at a time, revealing the hidden details that bring the stones around Notre Dame to life. Welcome, and let's explore together!

Our Expert Storytellers

Hailing from the vibrant state of Gujarat, India, Gourav brings a unique blend of warmth and historical insight to his tours. His passion for Parisian culture is contagious, and he excels at making every visitor feel like a local. Gourav's tours are known for their engaging details and friendly atmosphere.

Join Saurabh for a tour that is both educational and inspiring.
