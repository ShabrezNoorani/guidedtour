A Collective of Passionate Storytellers

Scenic Zest is more than just a tour company; it's a platform for local experts and passionate storytellers to share their love for Paris. We believe the best tours come from the heart. That's why we're proud to empower the next generation of talent by offering a space for student guides to host their own unique tours at a low commission.

Meet Our Team

Shabrez

Lead Guide & Storyteller

Originally from Kashmir, my deep love for history found a home on the ancient streets of Paris. When I'm not guiding, I'm writing blog posts to share more of the city's secrets. This, combined with a passion for marketing, is all about connecting people to the incredible stories that make Paris magical.

Saurabh

Guide & Architecture Enthusiast

Hailing from Gujarat, Saurabh's journey to Paris was a pilgrimage to the heart of art and architecture. He possesses a keen eye for the intricate details of Gothic design and loves seeing a visitor's face light up when they finally understand the story behind a sculpture or a stained-glass window.

Gourav

Marketing & Community Outreach

Gourav is the strategic mind dedicated to sharing the stories of Paris with travelers around the world. He builds partnerships and uses his digital expertise to make it easier for you to discover our tours. He believes authentic local experiences can transform a trip into an unforgettable journey.

Ifra Mir

Social Media & Community

Ifra is the visual storyteller who brings our tours to life on your screen. Through captivating photos and videos, she shares daily glimpses of the city's charm on social media, building a vibrant community of Paris lovers. Her goal is to make you feel the magic of Paris long before you even arrive.
