Tours & Bookings

Yes, a good guided tour is absolutely worth it. It saves you time, provides historical context you would otherwise miss, and helps you navigate busy sites with ease. A passionate guide can transform a simple visit into an unforgettable story.

General Paris Tips

Paris is extremely walkable. Exploring different neighborhoods (arrondissements) on foot is one of the best ways to discover its charm. For longer distances, the Métro is very efficient.

Yes, you can easily get by with English in most tourist areas, hotels, and restaurants. However, learning a few French pleasantries like 'Bonjour' (Hello), 'Merci' (Thank you), and 'S'il vous plaît' (Please) is highly appreciated and will make your interactions more pleasant.

The best times are spring (April-June) and fall (September-October) for pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer is popular but can be hot and crowded, while winter offers a festive atmosphere with fewer tourists.

Avoid assuming everyone speaks English (always start with 'Bonjour'), wearing flashy jewelry in crowded areas, and eating near major tourist traps. Also, remember to greet shopkeepers when you enter and leave a store.
